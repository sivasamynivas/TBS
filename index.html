Notes and corrections

What the estimating team asks the reader to keep in mind.

  • The shed is calculated as 630 sq.ft × ₹480 per sq.ft = ₹3,02,400.
  • Plastering cement is set at 40 bags, giving a plastering total of ₹32,800.
  • The SYNTAX water tank (₹15,000) is an additional cost, separate from electrical and plumbing.
  • Transportation, lift setup and miscellaneous are ₹10,000 each.
  • Use comparative quotations for steel, cement, bricks, tiles, doors, UPVC, electrical and plumbing materials.
  • Check structural quantities against approved drawings and reinforcement schedules before work starts.
  • Final spend can change with market rates, taxes, design revisions, site conditions, transport and labour availability.
  • Quantities stay as supplied in the project scope. Where both a quantity and a rate were given, the product is used.
